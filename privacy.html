Privacy Policy

Last updated:

1. What We Collect

  • Account data: name, email, and (if you provide it) age, mobile number, college, fields and subjects.
  • Learning activity: courses viewed, lessons and quizzes started/completed, progress and scores.
  • Technical data: a coarse device summary (operating system, browser, and whether the device is mobile).

2. What We Do NOT Collect

We do not store your IP address. Device information is limited to a non-identifying summary derived from your browser's user-agent string; any IP-like value is automatically redacted if it ever appears.

3. How We Use Data

To operate your account, personalize learning, surface progress/analytics, and keep the platform secure and reliable. Activity records are append-only and use server-generated timestamps.

4. Children & Guardians

For users under 18, guardian consent may be required before the account is fully activated, depending on the applicable policy configured by the platform administrator.

5. Legal Basis & Your Rights

Depending on your region, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Contact the platform administrator to exercise them.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data while your account is active. Activity records may be retained for auditing and analytics, then deleted or anonymized.

7. Security

Authorization is enforced server-side via row-level security. Access to personal data is limited to you, your instructors (only for students in their courses), and administrators.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be communicated through the Service.
